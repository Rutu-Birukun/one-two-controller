# Mechanical concept

## Current direction
The first enclosure should prioritize access, serviceability, and testability over polished industrial design.

## Recommended concept
- use a compact rectangular form factor
- place the display and controls on the front face
- keep the battery and audio hardware accessible for debugging
- reserve room for a USB-C connector and likely wiring routes
- leave space for a simple internal mounting strategy

## Prototype approach
- 3D-printed or machined prototype enclosure
- large enough to support board rework and service access
- clear panel labels for controls and output connectors

## Design considerations
- spacing for module replacement
- thermal visibility of amplifier and regulator
- ventilation or airflow if required
- fit with internal cable routing and battery placement
