# Audio test plan

## Objective
Verify that the audio output path is working before deeper firmware features are added.

## Checklist
- confirm DAC and amplifier power sequencing
- validate volume control at low and high settings
- test speaker and headphone output paths separately
- check for audible noise or distortion under load
- confirm mute behavior and gain stability

## Pass criteria
- output is stable with no clipping or excessive noise
- playback can start, pause, and stop reliably
- audio remains clear across expected volume range
