# Component Notes

## Candidate Priorities

- MCU: choose a part with strong firmware support and low complexity
- Audio: select a codec/amplifier path that matches the target enclosure and output style
- Power: ensure charging, regulation, and battery safety are reviewed together
- Storage: confirm what external media is realistic for the final use case

## Questions for Component Selection

- What output power is required for the target speaker or headphone experience?
- What is the required runtime?
- Is battery charging part of the final product or only a lab prototype requirement?
- How much board space is available in the final concept?
- Is this a device intended for indoor stationary use or portable operation?

## Recommendation

Keep the BOM intentionally flexible while the architecture is still being decided. Avoid locking in expensive or specialized parts before the requirements are stable.
