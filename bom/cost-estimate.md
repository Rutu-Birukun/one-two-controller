# Cost estimate

## Rough prototype budget

This is not a final production cost estimate. It is a rough planning target for prototype hardware and firmware iteration.

- MCU and support parts: $10-$25
- Audio DAC and amplifier: $5-$20
- Battery and charging: $8-$20
- Storage and connectors: $5-$15
- Display and controls: $5-$20
- Passive components and PCB fabrication: $15-$40
- Enclosure and mechanical prototype: $10-$30

## Total planning range

A small board-level prototype is likely in the range of $50-$150 depending on display, enclosure, and regulator choices.

## Cost-reduction guidance

- Use a common MCU and a simple audio path
- Keep the first revision to one storage strategy
- Avoid ultra-small mechanical integration if the board is still being validated
- Reuse development-friendly breakout modules where appropriate
