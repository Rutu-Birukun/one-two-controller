# Vendor and sourcing notes

## Recommended sourcing strategy
- Prefer common electronic distributors for first-pass prototyping
- Keep the BOM aligned with parts that are easy to source in small volumes
- Review alternatives for any component with lead-time risk

## Candidate vendors
- DigiKey
- Mouser
- JLCPCB parts library
- Adafruit for breakout-friendly modules
- SparkFun for prototyping-friendly boards and modules

## Notes for part review
- Check battery protection and charging IC compatibility before locking a chemistry
- Confirm amplifier and DAC pairing supports the target output type
- Review power regulator efficiency and thermal performance under expected load
- Prefer common MCU package variants with good community support
