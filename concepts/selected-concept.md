# Concept Notes

## Initial Concept

The project is currently a compact MP3-oriented controller concept, intended to merge a practical audio playback device with simple control and navigation.

## Future Concept Directions

- compact pocket-style player
- controller with separate amplifier stage
- device with tactile UI and minimal display
- hybrid controller and audio module concept
- portable playback device with modular case design

## Recommendation

Document the selected concept in this folder as it becomes final. Keep prototypes and speculative ideas here rather than in the core documentation set.
